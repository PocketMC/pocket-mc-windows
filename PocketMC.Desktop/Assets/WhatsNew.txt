VERSION=1.9.9.5

[NEW FEATURES]
Read PocketMC announcements and service notices in the new News page. News syncs in the background, stays cached for offline reading, and can show unread popup notices.

[IMPROVEMENTS]
Manage local Ollama models in separate Installed, Download, and Manual tabs. Pull recommended or custom models with progress and cancellation, and delete local models you no longer use.
PocketMC checks the Playit agent at startup and before connecting. If the runtime is incompatible, PocketMC attempts a verified repair while keeping your saved agent credentials.
Existing flat settings and version 1 PocketMC backups are converted to the organized format without resetting saved appearance or credentials.

[FIXES & STABILITY]
A window saved as maximized now reopens maximized instead of sometimes opening as a large, normal window.
Updating settings no longer replaces an existing backdrop, accent color, or wallpaper path with migration defaults.
