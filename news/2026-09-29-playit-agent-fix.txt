title: Playit Agent Stuck at Ready

subtitle: A quick fix for affected PocketMC 1.9.9 installations

heading: What happened

paragraph:
Some users may see their Playit agent remain at Ready without connecting after updating PocketMC.

paragraph:
The Playit executable is stored with PocketMC user data and is downloaded separately from the application. Updating PocketMC did not always replace an older Playit executable already on the computer.

heading: Quick fix

paragraph:
If your agent is stuck at Ready, remove the old local Playit executable from the Tunnel page, download it again, and press Connect.

list:
- Open the Tunnel page in PocketMC
- Select Delete Agent to remove only the local executable
- Select Download Agent to download it again
- Press Connect and keep your saved agent credentials

warning:
You do not need to delete your Playit agent or credentials from your Playit account for this workaround.

heading: Permanent fix

paragraph:
PocketMC is being updated to check the installed Playit executable at startup and repair an outdated copy automatically while preserving your credentials.
